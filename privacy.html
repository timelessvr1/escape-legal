ESCAPE Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

Developer: ESCAPE Studio ("we", "us")

Contact: escapevr.support@gmail.com

ESCAPE is a multiplayer virtual reality game for Meta Quest. This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and what choices you have. ESCAPE is intended for players aged 13 and older.

What we collect

From your Meta account (with your permission through Meta):

  • Your Meta user ID, used to create your ESCAPE account and keep your progress tied to you.
  • Your Meta display name, used as your default player name.
  • Whether you own the game (an entitlement check), so only owners can play online.

Created when you play:

  • Your player name, if you choose one in the game. Names are checked by a filter.
  • Game progress: your stash, items, in-game money (DaMoney), level, quests, skills, cosmetics, and hideout layout.
  • Gameplay statistics: kills, raids played and survived, and similar counts. Some of these appear on the in-game leaderboards with your player name.
  • Room activity: which game room you join, so friends can play together.

Technical information:

  • Your IP address and connection details, which are needed to connect your headset to our game servers. We do not use your IP address to identify you, and our servers do not keep it after your session beyond short-lived system logs used to keep the service running and secure.
  • Crash and performance reports (for example frame rate, errors, and which in-game objects were involved in a crash) that help us fix problems. These do not include your name or contact details.

Voice chat:

  • When voice chat is on, your voice is sent in real time through our servers to the other players in your game. We do not record or store voice chat. You can mute yourself and other players in the game.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not show third-party advertising or share your information with advertisers.
  • We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, location, or payment details. Purchases, if any, are handled by Meta.

How we use information

  • To run the game, save your progress, and let you play with other people.
  • To show leaderboards.
  • To prevent cheating and abuse, and to enforce the game's rules (for example acting on reports of offensive names or behaviour).
  • To fix bugs and improve performance.

Who we share it with

  • Other players see your player name, your avatar, leaderboard stats, and hear your voice when voice chat is on.
  • Service providers that host our servers (currently OVHcloud, in the United States) process data only to run the game for us.
  • Meta, as the platform the game runs on, under Meta's own privacy policy.
  • Authorities, if required by law.

How long we keep it

  • Account and progress data are kept while your account is active. Seasonal wipes reset progress on the schedule announced in the game.
  • Server system logs are kept for a short period (normally no more than 30 days).
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time (see below).

Your choices and rights

  • You can change your player name in the game, mute voice chat, and mute other players.
  • You can request a copy of your data or ask us to delete it by emailing escapevr.support@gmail.com from the address linked to your Meta account, or by including your in-game player name. We will respond within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law (such as the GDPR or CCPA); contact us to use them.

Children

ESCAPE is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 is using the game, contact us and we will delete their data.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, including key-only access to our servers and keeping server secrets out of the game and our code repository. No system is perfectly secure.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell players in the game.
